Digital Content

Sharing authentic campus stories across Instagram, LinkedIn, and Xiaohongshu — in our own words, not marketing copy.

This is the program where the work actually shows up publicly — real moments from tours, Open Days, and ambassador life, posted and captioned by the ambassadors themselves rather than run through an official marketing pass. If you want to see what SAA does before applying, this is where to look.

The same stories get shared across three channels with different audiences — Instagram, LinkedIn, and Xiaohongshu — cross-posted in the same honest voice rather than tailored into three different marketing pitches.
