What We Do

Community Events

Training, bonding, and ambassador-exclusive socials that keep the team close and the work sustainable.

The other five programs are all public-facing — this one isn't. Community Events is the internal side of SAA: training on campus knowledge and how to represent Sunway publicly, plus the bonding and socials that keep a volunteer-driven team functioning over time.

Public-facing work is only sustainable if the people doing it actually want to keep showing up. This program exists to make sure that stays true.

  • Training sessions on campus knowledge and tour delivery
  • Bonding activities and socials exclusive to ambassadors
  • Keeping the team close so the public-facing work stays sustainable
