Who We Are

A community built on being the person you needed when you were choosing a university.

Every prospective student who visits Sunway, messages a hashtag, or sits through an open-day talk deserves an honest answer from someone who's actually lived the experience — not a brochure. That's the role SAA exists to fill: a bridge between the university and the students still deciding whether Sunway is right for them.

01

Represent

We're often the first real conversation a prospective student or parent has with Sunway — at fairs, tours, and events on and off campus.

02

Guide

From application questions to "what's it actually like here," we give direct, first-hand answers instead of generic ones.

03

Connect

We keep the story going after the tour ends — through Instagram, LinkedIn, and the communities we run alongside it.

What We Do

The work happens in rooms, on paths, and in feeds.

Campus Tours

Walking prospective students and parents through campus life, faculties, and the details a map can't show.

Open Days & Fairs

Representing Sunway at recruitment events and school visits — see the Open Day Series below for our flagship one.

Peer Mentorship

One-on-one and group conversations that ease the transition for future students, before and after they enrol.

Digital Content

Sharing authentic campus stories across Instagram, LinkedIn, and Xiaohongshu — in our own words, not marketing copy.

Orientation Support

Helping new students settle in during their first weeks, when the questions are small but the stakes feel large.

Community Events

Training, bonding, and ambassador-exclusive socials that keep the team close and the work sustainable.

Flagship Campaign

Sunway Education Open Day Series

Open Day is where most prospective students meet Sunway in person — and where SAA does its most visible work. Each series, we recruit and brief a team of ambassadors to run booths, lead campus tours, and answer questions on the ground.

Become an Ambassador
